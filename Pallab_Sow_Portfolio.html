About Pallab

Pallab is a Master's in Social Work graduate from Visva-Bharati, Santiniketan — one of India's premier institutes for social work education — driven by a long-standing aim to work with vulnerable and underserved communities, and has spent the last two-plus years turning that training into real, on-ground delivery. His work has run across a central government drinking-water mission, corporate CSR execution, and a state government welfare project, giving him a rare mix of grassroots empathy and process discipline.

His academic training also sharpened his research and communication abilities — conducting research studies, preparing position papers and essays, and presenting his work at student conferences and seminars.

Alongside fieldwork, Pallab has built a working layer of AI tools, generative AI, SQL & BI, and machine learning skills through certified programmes with Google Cloud, Microsoft, Skill India and Reliance Foundation — so he brings data literacy into sectors that badly need it: MIS, MERL, and programme reporting.

He is fluent across English, Hindi and Bengali, based in Barasat, West Bengal, and open to roles in development, CSR, ESG, programme coordination or MERL.
